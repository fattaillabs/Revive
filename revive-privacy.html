Privacy Policy

Re:vive — Last updated: September 4, 2026

Our commitment: your conversations are stored only on your device. Re:vive is built with a local-first architecture — your imported chats, memories, and settings never leave your device for storage. AI replies can be generated in one of two ways: entirely on-device using Apple Intelligence, or — only with your explicit consent — through our Cloud AI service powered by Google Gemini. Cloud AI is optional, clearly disclosed before first use, and can be turned off at any time in Settings.

1. Data That Stays on Your Device

Re:vive stores all of your data locally on your device. The following data is created and stored only on your device and is never uploaded to or stored on our servers:

  • Imported chat histories — conversations you bring in from WhatsApp, KakaoTalk, LINE, or a screen recording of iMessage/SMS are parsed and stored locally.
  • AI-generated conversations — Your chat history within Re:vive is stored locally.
  • Conversation memory and embeddings — Vector databases used for memory recall are created and stored locally. Embedding generation always happens on-device. For some languages, Apple's on-device language model used for this is downloaded from Apple the first time it is needed — only the model comes down, and none of your conversation goes up.
  • Style profiles and relationship data — Analysis of conversation patterns remains on your device.
  • User preferences and settings — Your name, preferences, and app settings are stored locally using standard iOS storage.

2. Bringing In Your Conversations

Re:vive can bring in a conversation in two ways. Either way, what you hand over is read on your device and is never uploaded to us.

  • Exported chat files — a .txt or .zip export from WhatsApp, KakaoTalk, or LINE. You can pick the file inside Re:vive, or send it to Re:vive from another app's share sheet.
  • Screen recordings — Apple does not let apps export iMessage or SMS history, so for those you record your Messages screen and hand Re:vive the video. Re:vive reads the text off the video frames on your device using Apple's built-in text recognition. The video itself is never uploaded anywhere. Re:vive keeps only the text it read — its copy of the video is deleted as soon as reading finishes, and a copy left behind by an interrupted import is cleared the next time you open the app.
  • Your photo library — when you pick a recording, iOS shows its own picker and hands Re:vive only the one item you chose. Re:vive cannot browse or see the rest of your library.
  • Text read from a recording is treated exactly like any other imported chat — stored on your device, and, if you have turned Cloud AI on, small relevant excerpts may be sent to generate a reply (see Section 3).

3. Cloud AI (Optional, Consent-Based)

Re:vive can generate replies using Google Gemini through our secure relay server ("Cloud AI"). This mode is off until you explicitly agree to it in the app, and you can turn it off at any time in Settings → AI Engine. When Cloud AI is enabled:

  • What is sent: the message you type, recent messages from your current Re:vive chat, photos you explicitly choose to share in a chat (resized on-device before sending), and small excerpts of your imported conversation that are relevant to the reply (selected on-device), together with persona style instructions. This is the minimum needed to generate a reply.
  • What is never sent: your full imported chat archive, your contacts, your photo library (only individual photos you pick to share are sent), or anything from conversations you are not actively chatting in.
  • How it is used: solely to generate the reply. Content is processed transiently and is not stored on our server. Our server logs contain only technical metadata (status codes, message counts) — never your message content.
  • No AI training: requests are processed via Google's paid API tier, which per Google's API terms does not use your data to train AI models.
  • Security: all data is transmitted over encrypted connections (TLS).
  • If you decline or turn Cloud AI off, replies are generated entirely on-device using Apple Intelligence (on supported devices), and nothing is sent to any AI service.

4. iCloud Backup

If you have iCloud enabled on your device, Re:vive may back up your conversation data to your personal iCloud account. This is handled entirely by Apple's iCloud infrastructure.

  • iCloud backups are encrypted and managed by Apple.
  • We do not have access to your iCloud data.
  • You can disable iCloud backup for Re:vive in your device's Settings.

For more information on how Apple handles iCloud data, please refer to Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. Third-Party Advertising

Re:vive displays ads to free-tier users through Google AdMob. In connection with serving ads, the following may apply:

  • Google AdMob may collect device identifiers, IP address, and general usage data for ad personalization and analytics.
  • Ad data collection is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • You can limit ad tracking through your device's Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Premium subscribers do not see ads, and no ad-related data collection occurs for premium users.

6. Subscriptions & Purchases

Premium subscriptions and purchases are processed by Apple and managed with RevenueCat, a subscription management service. We never see or handle your payment details — those stay with Apple.

  • RevenueCat receives your purchase and subscription status (for example, which plan is active and when it renews) along with a randomly generated app user ID and basic device information.
  • This ID is not linked to your name, email, or any contact information, and we do not use it to identify you personally.
  • This is used only to unlock Premium features, restore your purchases on a new device, and keep your subscription status accurate.
  • Apple may send subscription events (renewals, cancellations, refunds) to RevenueCat on our behalf so your access stays correct even when the app is closed.

7. Data We Do NOT Collect

To be clear, we do not collect, store, or retain:

  • Your imported conversations, whatever app they came from
  • The screen recordings you import — the video never leaves your device, and Re:vive's copy is deleted after it is read
  • Messages you send or receive within Re:vive (when Cloud AI is enabled, message content is processed transiently to generate a reply and is never stored — see Section 3)
  • Names, phone numbers, or contact information
  • Photos, media, or attachments
  • Your location data
  • Your payment details (card numbers, billing information) — these are handled entirely by Apple
  • Any personal information beyond what is described above

8. On-Device AI Processing

When Cloud AI is off (the default until you consent), Re:vive uses Apple Intelligence (on-device Foundation Models) to generate AI responses. This means:

  • All AI processing happens on your device — nothing is sent to external servers.
  • No conversation data leaves your device for AI processing.
  • Apple's on-device models run without an internet connection.

On devices that support Apple Intelligence, on-device processing is also used as an automatic fallback whenever Cloud AI is unavailable (for example, offline).

9. Children's Privacy

Re:vive is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

10. Your Rights (EEA, UK, and Elsewhere)

Re:vive is available worldwide. Wherever you live, most of your data never leaves your device, so there is little for us to hand over — but the rights below still apply to what we do handle.

  • Who is responsible — FatTail Labs is the data controller for Re:vive. You can reach us at fattaillabs.ceo@gmail.com.
  • Why we may process data — Cloud AI runs on your consent, which you give before first use and can withdraw any time in Settings → AI Engine. Ads and subscription handling rest on our legitimate interest in operating a free tier and on performing the contract you entered when you subscribed.
  • Your rights — you may ask us for access to, correction of, deletion of, or a copy of any personal data we hold about you, object to or restrict how we use it, and withdraw consent at any time. Write to the address above and we will answer within one month.
  • Deleting your data yourself — because everything is local, deleting the app removes your imported chats, memories, and settings from your device. If iCloud backup is on, remove Re:vive's data in your device's Settings → iCloud as well.
  • Where data goes — when Cloud AI is on, the small excerpts described in Section 3 travel to our relay server hosted on Google Cloud in Seoul, South Korea, and from there to Google's Gemini API. These transfers outside the EEA and the UK rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses that Google Cloud applies to its customers. Content is processed transiently and is not stored.
  • How long we keep things — we keep no conversation content at all. Server logs hold only technical metadata (status codes, message counts) and are kept no longer than our hosting provider's default log retention, currently 30 days. Purchase records held by RevenueCat last as long as your subscription plus the period Apple and tax law require.
  • Complaints — if you are in the EEA or the UK you may complain to your local data protection authority. We would rather hear from you first so we can put things right.
